Privacy Policy, Fridgefolk

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fridgefolk ("we", "us") handles information when

you use the Fridgefolk mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree

to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and display name, created when you

sign up with email/password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple.

  • Profile picture: an optional avatar you choose from a set of built-in icons. The App does not upload or store photos from your device.
  • Pantry content: the boards you create or join, the food items you add (names, quantities, expiry dates), and invite codes. When you scan a barcode, a product name and image may be fetched from a public product database and stored with the item; this image comes from that database, not from your camera or photo library.
  • Messages: the text and activity you post in a board's shared thread.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic device and diagnostic data needed to run the App and keep it

reliable (for example, crash information).

Camera

  • The App uses your camera only to scan barcodes and read expiry dates. These images are processed on your device to read the text or barcode and are not uploaded or stored. The App does not upload your camera feed, and it does not access your photo library.

We do not collect payment information (the App is free), and we do not sell

your personal information.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To sync your boards, items, and messages across the members of a board.
  • To send you notifications you enable (for example, expiry reminders).
  • To operate, maintain, debug, and improve the App.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and keep the community safe.

3. How your information is shared

  • With other members of a board you belong to. Anything you add to a shared

board, items, photos attached to items, your display name and avatar, and your

messages, is visible to the other members of that board. Only share a board's

invite code with people you trust.

  • With service providers. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud

Firestore) to run the App's accounts and data sync. Their handling of data is

governed by Google's privacy policy.

  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect users' safety.

We do not otherwise share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • We keep your information for as long as your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time from within the App

(Profile → Delete account). Deleting your account removes your user profile

and removes you from your boards. Boards with no remaining members are deleted

along with their items.

  • Messages and item entries you created may retain the display name shown at the

time they were written, as a record of board activity, but are no longer linked

to a live account after deletion.

  • You may also request deletion by emailing fridgefolkapp@gmail.com.

5. Age requirement and children's privacy

The App is for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly

collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18

has provided us information, contact fridgefolkapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including authenticated access and server-side

security rules, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is

100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete

your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these

rights, use the in-app controls or email fridgefolkapp@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated"

date above and, for material changes, notify you in the App.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at fridgefolkapp@gmail.com.
