Sharing food is easy. Talking about it is the hard part.

The yoghurt that expired last week. The onions nobody bought more of. The leftovers with no owner. It all ends up as an awkward group chat, or worse, a face to face nudge that lands badly.

●"Whose is this and can it go?"
●"We are out of milk again."
●"That has been in there for two weeks."
How it works

Three easy things.

Set up a shared pantry in under a minute. No accounts for the whole house to manage, no spreadsheets.

Start a pantry

Create a board for your place and share the invite code. You approve who joins, so only your housemates get in.

Add what is inside

Scan a barcode or type it in. Fridgefolk tracks the expiry and shows you at a glance what is fresh, what to use soon, and what has gone off.

Talk about the food

Every pantry has its own thread, tied to the actual items. Sort out the expired and the almost gone without making it weird.

What is inside

Everything a shared kitchen needs.

Small, useful, and built for the way a house actually runs.

See what is going off

A freshness bar sorts everything into fresh, use soon, and expired, so nothing rots at the back of the shelf.

Add items in seconds

Scan a barcode and the name fills itself in. Add the expiry and you are done. No more typing every carton by hand.

You choose who joins

Invite by code, approve every request. Your pantry stays your people.

Gentle reminders

An optional daily nudge tells you what needs eating before it is a bin job.

Calm and in your control

Report or block anyone, delete your account and data whenever you want. Your data lives with your housemates and no one else.

Activity that reads like a story

"Sam used the spinach" shows up right in the thread, so the chat and the food stay in one place.

The part that makes it work

A neutral place to nudge, so it never feels like a callout.

Asking about someone's food to their face is awkward. In Fridgefolk the message sits next to the item, addressed to the food and not the person. It is easier to say, and easier to hear.

Made to feel calm, not like a chore.

Get everyone on the same page.

Fridgefolk is free. Set up your pantry, invite the people you share a fridge with, and let the fridge sort itself out.
